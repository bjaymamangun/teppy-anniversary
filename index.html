Happy 1st Anniversary, love!

One year. I honestly can't believe how fast time has passed. It feels like just yesterday when we had our Baguio trip, not knowing that those moments would become the beginning of something so special between us.

I know I'm not the best when it comes to expressing my feelings through words. Sometimes, I struggle to show how much I care, and I know there are moments when I can do better. But I want you to know that I appreciate you more than I probably say. Thank you for your love, patience, understanding, and for being someone I get to share my life with.

As we celebrate our first year together, one thing I want you to know is that I will always be rooting for you. I hope you achieve every dream and goal you've been working hard for. I hope you get everything you've been wishing and praying for, not just for yourself but also for your family. I know how much they mean to you, and I genuinely wish to see you succeed and be happy.

And through all of it, I want you to know that I'll be right beside you. Whether things are going well or life gets difficult, I'll be here to support you, cheer for you, and remind you that you don't have to face everything alone. I may not have all the answers or know exactly what to do all the time, but I'll always do my best to be someone you can count on.

I know one year is just the beginning, and there's still so much for us to experience. More trips, more adventures, more random conversations, more laughter, and more memories to look forward to. I hope we continue growing together, learning from each other, and choosing each other even on the days when things aren't easy.

Thank you for coming into my life, for choosing me, and for making this past year so memorable. If there's one thing I'm certain of, it's that I'm happy that our paths crossed.

Here's to our first year and to all the years ahead of us.

Happy Anniversary, my love!

I love you, Teppy. Always. ♡

Yours,
